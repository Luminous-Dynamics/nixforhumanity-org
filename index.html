Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Luminous Nix

Luminous Nix is a natural language interface for NixOS that makes package management accessible to everyone. Instead of learning complex commands, you can simply tell your computer what you want in plain English (or any language). It's like having a friendly expert who translates your requests into NixOS commands.

Luminous Nix sits on top of NixOS and provides a conversational interface. You still get all the benefits of NixOS (reproducibility, rollbacks, declarative configuration), but you don't need to memorize commands or read extensive documentation. It's the same powerful system, just 10,000x more accessible.

Yes! Luminous Nix is completely free and open source under the MIT License. We believe powerful tools should be accessible to everyone. You can view the source code, contribute improvements, or even fork the project on GitHub.

Not at all! That's the whole point. Luminous Nix is designed for people who don't know NixOS. Whether you're a complete beginner, someone with ADHD who finds traditional CLIs overwhelming, or an experienced user who wants a faster workflow, Luminous Nix adapts to your needs.

Luminous Nix supports natural language input in multiple languages including English, Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Japanese, and more. You can even mix languages in the same conversation! The AI understands context and intent, not just keywords.

The package operations themselves are just as fast (they use the same underlying NixOS engine). But the time to productivity is 10,000x faster because you don't need to spend weeks learning commands, reading documentation, or debugging syntax errors. Most users accomplish their first task in minutes instead of hours.

Absolutely. Luminous Nix processes your commands locally on your machine. We don't collect or send your data to any external servers (unless you explicitly opt-in to anonymous usage statistics). Your privacy and security are our top priorities. Read our Privacy Policy for details.

Yes! Luminous Nix supports voice input through your browser's speech recognition API. This makes it accessible for users with motor disabilities, vision impairments, or anyone who prefers speaking to typing. Just click the microphone icon and start talking.

Getting started is easy! First, make sure you have NixOS installed. Then install Luminous Nix with one command: nix-env -iA nixpkgs.luminous-nix. After that, just type ask-nix "help me get started" and the AI will guide you through the rest. Check our tutorial for detailed instructions.
