❄️ Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 14, 2025

Our Commitment to Privacy

At Luminous Nix, we respect your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you interact with nixforhumanity.org.

TL;DR: We collect minimal information (only what you provide via forms), use it solely for the beta program, never sell your data, and you can request deletion anytime.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

When you sign up for our beta program, we collect:

  • Name: To personalize communications
  • Email address: To contact you about the beta program
  • NixOS experience level: To better understand our community
  • Use case (optional): To improve Luminous Nix for your needs

Automatically Collected Information

We do NOT use tracking cookies or analytics on this website. We may receive basic server logs from GitHub Pages (our hosting provider), which include:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited
  • Timestamp of visits

These logs are managed by GitHub and subject to GitHub's Privacy Policy.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively for:

  1. Beta Program Management: Sending you beta access, updates, and important announcements
  2. Product Improvement: Understanding user needs to make Luminous Nix more accessible
  3. Communication: Responding to your questions or feedback

We will NEVER:

  • Sell your information to third parties
  • Use your data for advertising
  • Share your information without your consent (except as required by law)
  • Send you spam or unrelated marketing

Third-Party Services

Formspree (Form Processing)

Our beta signup form is processed by Formspree. When you submit the form, your data passes through their service to reach us. Formspree's privacy policy is available at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

Ko-fi (Donations)

If you choose to support us via Ko-fi, that transaction is handled by Ko-fi and subject to their privacy policy. We receive notification of your support but no payment details.

GitHub (Hosting & Sponsors)

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. If you sponsor us on GitHub, that's managed by GitHub under their privacy policy.

Data Storage and Security

Your beta signup information is stored securely via Formspree's infrastructure. We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data, including:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmission
  • Limited access to submitted information
  • No storage of sensitive payment information (handled by third parties)

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the information we have about you
  • Correction: Request corrections to your information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your information at any time
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from beta program communications

To exercise these rights, contact us through Luminous Dynamics or reply to any email we send you.

Children's Privacy

Luminous Nix is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify beta participants of significant changes via email. The "Last Updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.

International Users

Luminous Nix is operated from the United States. By using our services, you consent to the transfer of your information to the United States and processing according to this privacy policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or our data practices, please contact:

  • Website: luminousdynamics.org
  • GitHub: Luminous-Dynamics
Accessibility Note: This privacy policy is written in plain language to be accessible to all users. If you need this information in an alternative format, please contact us.
